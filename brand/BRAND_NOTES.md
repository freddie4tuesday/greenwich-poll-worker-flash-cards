# Greenwich ROV brand notes

This is a summary, not the guide itself. The original brand identity guide and logo files are in the owner's Google Drive folder "Greenwich Brand Identity and Logos" (folder id `1IlJGAZaHqVZ755xeDil8NJ8G_fDrT9ut`). Check details against the original.

- **Primary color:** green `#1f4433`.
- **Secondary color:** gold `#b99c35`.
- **Cream:** `#faf9f5` is used in the owner's other tools. It was not confirmed from this guide.
- **Primary typeface:** Lato.
- **Secondary typeface:** Garamond, used sparingly as an accent. Use EB Garamond from Google Fonts on the web.

## Logo rules

- Use only the approved PNG files.
- Do not redraw, retype, recolor or distort the logo.
- Keep its proportions and leave clear space around it.
