# Source: Canva "Poll Worker Training Cards"

- **Design id:** `DAG0Y9rlhvQ`.
- **Link:** https://www.canva.com/d/k2Bos7mCQUs24_Y
- **Size:** 106 pages, each 240×336.

## Page structure

- **Odd pages:** the card back, solid green with the Greenwich ROV logo.
- **Even pages:** the card face, with a green question band, a white answer panel, and a green logo band.
- **The 52 cards** are the first 104 pages.
- **The last two pages** are the closing thank-you from the Registrars of Voters (Mary Hegarty, Fred DeCaro III).

## Topics covered

Polling place basics, the checker's table, ballot clerk duties, the tabulator tender, Moderator and SAR responsibilities, and early voting.
